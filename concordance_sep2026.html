Does the concordance signal hold on the corrected PPI table?

The concordance rate survives the corrected table almost unchanged (54/83, 65.1%). Whether it survives its strongest null is a different question, and the answer is no — a finding that was already latent in the April permutation output but never reported. §05 is the section that matters.

01 What changed in the input

The new table supersedes Final_cutshort_…_25AUG25.csv. It is not simply the old list with labels added.

  • 102 rows (was 92): 91 differential + 11 positive controls, now labelled explicitly as mssEffect == "insig" rather than left blank.
  • 10 pairs are genuinely new. Max's note listed 16, but 6 of those were already present — see the caveat in §05.
  • Nothing was dropped. Three pairs that appeared to vanish (PPP1CC-MCM4, SMARCA4-BCL7B, USP7-DNMT1) were simply rewritten with _ instead of -.
  • All 102 pairs are testable — every bait and prey is in the 113-gene library.

Regression check passed. The refactored pipeline reproduces the published 51/76 (67.1%, p=0.0019) exactly when pointed back at the old table, so the change in result comes from the input and nothing else.

03 Robustness

The full sweep: 3 normalisations × 3 metrics × 17 gene sets = 153 combinations. 45/153 reach p<0.05 (was 50/153). The result is not an artefact of one processing choice.

04 Does it hold at the guide level?

Each gene is knocked down by three separate sgRNAs. Splitting every knockdown into its individual guides turns each bait–prey pair into up to 3×3 = 9 independent correlations per condition. If the gene-level result were driven by one bad guide or one off-target effect, it would not survive this.

It survives. Across 581 guide combinations spanning 81 pairs, concordance is 345/581 (59.4%), and the confound-free contrast (up/gained vs down/lost) gives Mann–Whitney p=3.2e-04 — the same conclusion as the gene-level test, reached from 3× more measurements.

05 Permutation tests

Three nulls for the correlation result, each breaking something different. The shaded tail in every panel is the permutation p-value itself — the null mass at or beyond the observed statistic.

The result clears the two weaker nulls but not the strongest one. Against label shuffle (p=0.0102) and random gene pairs (p=0.0082) the 65.1% concordance is significant. Against the cell shuffle it is p=0.1079 — and for the up/gained arm, p=0.1284. Neither reaches significance.

The cell shuffle is the only null that rebuilds pseudobulk, HVG selection and the whole correlation matrix from randomised data. Doing so, it lands at 58.3% concordance with no perturbation identity whatsoever — already reproducing about 49% of the observed excess over the label-shuffle null. Whatever produces that 58.3% is structural, not biological: the global Vorinostat shift, HVG-selection instability and cell-count effects manufacture most of the apparent concordance on their own.

This was already true in April, and the number never propagated. The April 10,000-permutation run recorded perm3_cell_shuffle: p=0.115, z=1.34 in UpGained_Permutation_Union1K_summary.json — statistically the same answer found here. But ANALYSIS_README.md still cites an earlier 100-permutation run as “p<0.01, z=2.72”, and the published April robustness report omits the cell shuffle entirely. The stale figure has been carried forward ever since; 100 permutations cannot resolve a p-value at that level.

What still holds. The AP-MS labels do carry information: they beat a label permutation at p=0.0102 and arbitrary gene pairs at p=0.0082. The confound-free directional test — up/gained versus down/lost by Mann–Whitney, which cancels any global shift — gives p=0.00062. What cannot be claimed on this evidence is that the concordance rate exceeds what the assay produces from randomised perturbation identity.

A second, related correction: the up/gained baseline. Correlations rose globally under Vorinostat — 55 of 83 differential pairs have Δr > 0 regardless of AP-MS label. The correct null for “fraction of up/gained pairs that move up” is therefore 66.2%, not 50%, and the permutation reproduces that value exactly. Against 50% the arm looks decisive (binomial p=0.00008); against its own null it is p=0.0102. The one-sample Wilcoxon quoted in the paired-dot captions (p=3.97×10−6) is confounded the same way — it measures the drug's global effect, not the AP-MS effect.

The requested proof does not come out cleanly, and the reason is the control assignments. Across both top-10 lists, 3 of 20 pairs clear their control with a CI excluding zero — but 0 of the 6 pairs that have a genuinely non-changing control do. Every apparent win is measured against a denominator that is itself a changing PPI: PRMT1_MYCN sits at r=0.094 under Vorinostat, so PRMT1–PRKACA and PRMT1–STRBP score ratios of 2.29 against a control that has itself moved.

There is also a conceptual limit worth stating: “at or above the positive control” is the right expectation only for PPIs that are present or strengthened. For down/lost pairs, falling below the control is the AP-MS-consistent outcome — which is exactly what PIM1–MBD3 does (ratio 0.69, CI entirely below zero). Read as a one-sided test it is the strongest single result in the panel, but it supports the AP-MS call by moving the opposite way.

To make this test carry weight, the AP-MS side would need to designate one non-changing control per bait. Fourteen of 25 baits currently have none.

07 Caveats worth raising

Six of the “missing 16” were never missing. They were in the old table but silently dropped, because one partner fails knockdown QC in one condition: CUL3, RREB1, SHROOM3, SRGAP2 have no DMSO data; HTATSF1, KMT2D have no Vorinostat data. Eight of the 91 differential pairs drop this way, leaving 83 analysable. This is a data limitation, not a hand-off error.

YWHAE_YWHAG and YWHAG_YWHAE are the same unordered pair with different calls — up and insig respectively. AP-MS is directional so that is legitimate, but correlation is symmetric, so the pair is simultaneously a changing PPI and a non-changing control. Worth a check on the AP-MS side.

One concordant call is a coin flip. PRMT1_FAM184B has Δr = −6.9×10−5 — counted concordant, but that is 0.08% of its own correlation magnitude. It is one of the 54.
